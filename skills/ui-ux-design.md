# UI/UX Design Guidance

Guidelines for Summit Air Heating & Cooling (demo).

## 1. Visual Aesthetics & Tone
- **Industry & Vibe:** Professional, dependable, clean US residential HVAC service provider.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: Deep Navy/Slate (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`) representing stability and trust.
  - Accent / Action: Vibrant Cool Blue (`#0284c7` / `#0ea5e9`) & Warm Emergency Alert Orange (`#ea580c` / `#f97316`) for heating/emergency.
  - Backgrounds: Crisp clean whites (`#ffffff`) and soft tinted slate/gray (`#f8fafc`) with subtle borders (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif (Inter / system fonts), high legibility on mobile.
- **Contrast & Hierarchy:** Strong contrast meeting WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum). Clear visual hierarchy from H1 down to micro-copy.

## 2. Conversion Architecture
- **Primary Conversion Paths:**
  1. Direct Phone Call (Tap-to-call visible everywhere, desktop header + mobile fixed sticky bar).
  2. Short Service Request Form (< 60 seconds completion time).
- **Homeowner Anxiety Reducers:**
  - Clear emergency / same-day availability callouts.
  - Upfront service area list (so visitors immediately see if Summit Air services their zip code / city).
  - Clear licensing, bonding, insurance, and warranty badges.
  - Transparent pricing & free estimates guarantee.

## 3. Accessibility & Mobile Optimization
- Tap targets: Minimum 44x44px for all mobile interactive elements.
- Form inputs: Explicit `<label>` elements, large touch targets, accessible error states.
- High readability in bright outdoor or stressful low-light conditions.
- No jarring layout shifts (CLS = 0).
